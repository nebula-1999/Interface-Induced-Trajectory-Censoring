\section{Statistical estimands and retrospective test family}
\label{app:statistics}

\subsection{Finite-sample interval for the formal RL endpoint}
Let $p_+$ denote the probability that an evaluation item passes only under repaired FC and
$p_-$ the probability that it passes only under broken FC. The paired risk difference is
$\Delta=p_+-p_-$. At step 150, the counts are $(n_+,n_-,n)=(0,3,542)$.
For each discordance probability we invert the binomial distribution to obtain a two-sided
97.5\% Clopper--Pearson interval, denoted $[L_+,U_+]$ and $[L_-,U_-]$.
The union bound gives at least 95\% coverage for
$[L_+-U_-,\,U_+-L_-]$ under independent evaluation-item pairs. This conservative construction
does not rely on a normal approximation with three discordant pairs. The point difference is
$-0.55$ percentage points and the interval is $[-1.79,0.72]$ percentage points.
For repaired step 0 versus 150, $(1,4,542)$ gives $[-2.06,1.01]$ percentage points.
These are unadjusted endpoint intervals, not simultaneous intervals for the whole test family.
They quantify item-pair uncertainty under an independence assumption, not training-seed,
inference-retest, benchmark-selection or task-cluster uncertainty.

\subsection{Explicit reporting family}
This is a retrospective reporting audit, not a preregistered multiplicity policy.
The family includes all 15 metrics in the five audited Llama control contrasts, even when only
one metric is highlighted, plus the 12 distinct comparisons below. Repeated appearances of the
same contrast count once. Nominal $p$ values are exact two-sided McNemar tests;
adjusted $p=\min(1,27p)$, with threshold $0.05/27=0.00185185$.
Historical 1.5B motivating runs and abandoned/invalid runs are not confirmatory evidence and
are outside this family; their original disclosures remain in the historical audit.
The two both-parsed contrasts are retained in the family but remain post-treatment descriptive
comparisons, not causal effects. This explicit family replaces the earlier approximate count of
twelve; in particular, the Llama strict-to-ReAct and both-parsed contrasts no longer clear
the corrected threshold.

The accompanying \texttt{submission\_statistics.py} recomputes the 15 Llama tests from
item-level records and the remaining tests from the explicit paired counts, with their sources
identified in \texttt{submission\_statistics.json}. Thus nominal and adjusted values can be
reproduced without GPUs. The latter count-based recomputation is not a new item-level audit
of every historical comparison.

The later post-hoc native FC evaluation adds a separately identified nine-test
exploratory family (Appendix~\ref{app:nativefc}): three paired weight contrasts
crossed with final success, tool initiation and rescue. Its per-item inputs,
nominal and Bonferroni-adjusted values are supplied separately. No new contrast
is nominally significant, and none becomes significant if all 36 tests are pooled.
The historical 27-test audit is preserved, not retroactively called preregistered.
